FUTURE DEVELOPMENT

Air quality, temperature, contaminants and microorganisms matter in a closed spacecraft. ASTRA's current hardware has no sensor for them, so it shows no such values. For now you can note discomfort in the check-in. Environmental sensors are a possible later addition.
