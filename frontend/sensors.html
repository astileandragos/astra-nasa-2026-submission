Can I trust the readings?

MAX30102 · heart rate and SpO2
--

Heart rate and SpO2 are estimated from light on the fingertip. They need a steady finger and a still hand.

ICM-20948 · motion
--

Measures acceleration. ASTRA uses it for activity and rest only.

Missing or unavailable data, last 7 days

Missing values stay empty, and no health warning is made from a poor-signal reading.

How to get a good measurement

  1. Place your fingertip flat on the sensor and keep it there.
  2. Keep your hand and arm still while it measures.
  3. Check that the sensor and cable are firmly connected.
  4. Wait a few seconds and look again. Readings with a poor signal are not used for any warning.
